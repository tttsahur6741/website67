Earning Potential

In the United States, web developers enjoy a highly competitive compensation landscape, commanding a median annual salary ranging from approximately $92,650 to $101,000 according to recent data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and Glassdoor. Entry-level professionals generally start with salaries between $50,000 and $73,000, while mid-career positions quickly elevate closer to the national average. Earning potential peaks significantly for senior developers, specialized full-stack engineers, and those working in major tech hubs like California and Washington, where top-tier earners frequently pull in over $134,000 to $162,000 annually.
