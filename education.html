Education & Training

Based on the education data provided in the chart, a Bachelor's degree is the most common qualification for professionals in this field, held by 54% of workers. Higher education plays a dominant role overall, with 15% of employees earning a Master's degree and 1% holding a doctoral or professional degree. At the same time, the field accommodates several alternative paths to entry. A significant 16% of workers have some college experience but no formal degree, which is a common scenario for self-taught programmers or coding bootcamp graduates. Additionally, 9% of the workforce holds an Associate's degree, 4% entered with a high school diploma or equivalent, and only 1% have less than a high school diploma. Altogether, these statistics indicate that while independent or shorter training routes are entirely viable, over 70% of professionals secure their roles after completing a college degree program.
